Delaware Dark Sky

Welcome to Delaware Dark Sky!

A platform designed to track light polution across the state, provide users with the best locations for stargazing in their area, and advocate for dark sky preservation across Delaware.

What Does This App Do?

This app takes any Delaware address or ZIP code entered by the user and converts it into a specific coordinate point. The program then takes the travel radius entered by the user and finds all safely available stargazing locations within that area, providing information for all of them, and highlights the best option based on it's Bortle Darkness Score. Click the Map tab to try it out yourself!

My Inspiration

As someone who was born and raised in Delaware, i've encountered first hand the effects of light polltion brought on by rapid urbanization. Light Pollution Impacts on Ecosystems and Communities

Wildlife Displacement and the Atlantic Flyway

Delaware's coastal wetlands serve as critical migratory pitstops along the Atlantic Flyway, a major migration route for birds and wildlife in North America. Excessive artificial light disorients nocturnal migratory birds, disrupting their star-based navigation systems, forcing exhausting detours, and increasing fatalities. With increasing urbanzation across Delaware, it's more important now than ever to advocate for sheilded outdoor light fixtures, lower wattages, and community action. Find more information at darksky.org.

STEM Access Gap in Delaware Communities

Severe light pollution in metropolitan school districts blocks students from viewing the night sky, creating an educational barrier to foundational observational astronomy. By listing safe, protected, and nearby dark areas, this platform helps students utilize public spaces as natural astronomy classrooms.
